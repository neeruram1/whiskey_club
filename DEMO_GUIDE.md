# Collective Spirits - Demo Guide

## What is Collective Spirits?

Collective Spirits is a web app for managing a whiskey tasting club. It helps members:
- Track which whiskeys the club has tasted
- Rate and review bottles
- Discover which members have similar tastes
- Save bottles they want to drink again
- View club statistics and highlights

### 5. ⭐ **Rating a Bottle**
**How it works:**
- From a bottle or meeting page, click "Rate This Bottle"
- Select a score from 0-5 (with 0.5 increments)
- Add optional comment
- **Select flavor tags** - collapsible list of 19 options:
  - Smoky, Peaty, Fruity, Spicy, Sweet, Oaky, Floral, Nutty
  - Vanilla, Caramel, Citrus, Herbal, Honey, Chocolate, Leather
  - Tropical, Earthy, Maritime, Medicinal
- Click "Save Rating"
- **Automatic Attendance:** Rating a bottle automatically marks you as attending that meeting

**Demo Tips:**
- Show the flavor profile toggle button (expand/collapse the list)
- Explain that you can select multiple flavors
- Point out that ratings update the bottle's average immediately
- Show how your rating appears on your profile
- Mention that rating also marks attendance (no need to manually check a box)

## Common Questions & Answers

**Q: Do I have to rate bottles immediately at the tasting?**
A: No! You can rate anytime - during the tasting, later that night, or even days later.

**Q: Can I change my rating?**
A: Yes, you can edit your rating and comments anytime.

**Q: What if I don't know which flavors to pick?**
A: Flavors are optional! Just select what you notice. Common ones are Smoky, Sweet, Spicy, Fruity.

**Q: How do I add a bottle to Encore Pours?**
A: Just click the heart icon on any bottle page or meeting page.

**Q: What's a "Spirit Guide"?**
A: That's what we call the member who brings the bottle for a regular tasting. They're guiding us through that whiskey.

**Q: Can I see other people's ratings?**
A: Yes! On any bottle detail page, scroll down to see all member ratings.

**Q: How does taste compatibility work?**
A: It compares your ratings to someone else's for bottles you've both tried. Higher % means you rate things similarly.

**Q: What's a Flight Night?**
A: A special meeting where multiple members each bring a bottle - like a whiskey flight at a bar. Usually 3-5 bottles.
